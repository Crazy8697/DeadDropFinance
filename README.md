# Dead Drop Finance

Free, local-first envelope budgeting for Android, from [DarkRelay](https://darkrelay.net).

Give every dollar a job. Your budget lives on your phone, encrypted. There is no account to make, no ads and no
telemetry.

## Status

In development. There is no release yet.

## Requirements

Android 13 or newer.

## Privacy

Your budget stays in the app's private storage on your phone, encrypted. Bank connections go through your own
SimpleFIN or Teller account, never through DarkRelay. Backups go only where you choose. The app sends nothing about
you anywhere.
