Teller removed.

- Teller sync is gone. Teller doesn't offer public signups for independent developers, so new users couldn't get
  the Teller account it needed.
- If you had a Teller connection, it's removed when you update. Its transactions stay, as ordinary ones, and your
  accounts are unchanged.
- Bank sync is SimpleFIN (paid directly to SimpleFIN) or CSV import, which is free.
- A smaller download.
- Requires Android 13 or newer.
