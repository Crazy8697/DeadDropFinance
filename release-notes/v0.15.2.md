A few follow-ups to bank sync and updates.

- Linking: when you create a new account from a bank account, an "It's actually negative (overdrawn)" switch is
  there for banks that report an overdrawn balance as positive. Off by default.
- Bank sync asks SimpleFIN for 45 days at a time, as SimpleFIN recommends. A first 90-day sync is two requests.
- Updates: on mobile data, Download and install first tells you about how big the download is and asks.
- Requires Android 13 or newer.
