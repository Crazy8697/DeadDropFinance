Fixes from the first real bank sync.

- A long first sync (a year of history) no longer closes the app. Syncs now run in the background, one at a time
  per connection, save their progress as they go, and pick up where they stopped if they're interrupted. Any
  problem shows as a message on the connection instead.
- The bottom bar shows icons and the tab names Home, Envelopes, Activity and More.
- Unlinking a synced account asks whether to take it off the budget (the default) or keep tracking it by hand.
  Archiving any account takes its money off Ready to Assign, and unarchiving puts it back.
- Track only accounts: money that must never be budgeted, like a membership share. It shows and syncs but never
  counts in Ready to Assign.
- Held amounts: a minimum that stays in an account. Ready to Assign counts the balance minus it.
- Negative bank balances show their minus sign, in the warning color.
- The Finish setup card is gone once you're set up.
- Requires Android 13 or newer.
