A clearer receipt check, and receipts read more reliably.

- Each item on the receipt check is its own card: name, quantity, price, the price with tax beside it, Taxed or Not taxed, its envelope, and Checked above Remove. A quantity shows as the receipt does, for example 2 @ $2.79 = $5.58.
- The totals show as the receipt does: untaxed items, taxed items, tax, and total paid. When they don't add up, the total says by how much, and any price that one misread digit would explain is pointed out. Nothing is changed for you.
- Anything the reader wasn't sure of has a yellow border and says why, until you change it or mark the item Checked.
- A receipt you are checking stays as you left it if you go to another screen or close the app, until you save it or clear it. Clear throws the scan out after asking.
- Split by items and item returns use each item's price with tax.
- Section defaults work for any envelope names, and the receipt reader handles more of what a photo does to a receipt.
- Requires Android 13 or newer.
