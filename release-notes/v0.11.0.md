A new Home built around Safe to Spend, parked buys, and payday that runs by itself.

- Home: Safe to Spend is at the top, counting only your everyday envelopes (groceries and gas to start) until next
  payday. Tap it to change which envelopes count. Below it: the balance forecast, Ready to assign when money is
  waiting, Pay these now, transactions to sort, parked buys, and your everyday envelopes. Rollover isn't on Home.
- Parked buys: park something you want with its price and a wait of 1 day, 2 days or 1 week. After the wait it
  says whether Rollover has room. Buy it (from Rollover or another envelope), keep waiting, or drop it. Dropped
  amounts add up. The charge is sorted to the envelope you picked when it comes in. Set the default wait in More.
- Payday runs by itself when the payday deposit comes in through bank sync, a CSV import or a bank notification.
  The "It's payday" card is gone, and a deposit seen twice never runs payday twice.
- Requires Android 13 or newer.
