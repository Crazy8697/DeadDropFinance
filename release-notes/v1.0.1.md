Adds a Bonus income button for money that doesn't come through the bank.

- Bonus income: on Home, enter an amount, a date, how you received it (Cash, Gift card, Venmo or Other) and a note.
  It goes to Ready to Assign, isn't tied to any account and isn't a payday. It shows in Activity, where you can edit
  or delete it.
- Requires Android 13 or newer.
