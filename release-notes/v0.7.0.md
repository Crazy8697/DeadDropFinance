Setup and bank sync.

- A setup wizard on first start: app lock, how your transactions get in, and optional starter envelopes (names
  only). If you're updating with accounts already in the app, a Finish setup card on Home opens it instead.
  Rerun it any time from More, Setup.
- Bank sync, three ways in:
  - Nerdy: Teller, with your own free Teller developer account. You import the certificate Teller gives you and
    log in to your bank inside Teller's own page.
  - Easy: SimpleFIN Bridge, paid by you to SimpleFIN. Paste a setup token once.
  - Old School: CSV import and entering things yourself, as before.
- Synced transactions land in To sort. Ones you already entered by hand or imported from a CSV are matched, not
  doubled. Pending ones are updated in place when they post, keeping any envelope you chose.
- Each account shows its last sync and whether its balance matches the bank's. Nothing is adjusted for you.
- Syncs when you open the app, every few hours in the background (you can turn that off), and when you pull down on
  Home or Activity.
- Bank logins and credentials stay on your phone, encrypted with their own key. DarkRelay never holds your bank
  connection.
- There are still no backups. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, your budget is gone.
- Requires Android 13 or newer.
