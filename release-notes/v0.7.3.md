Account balances fixed, and two new ways to remove data.

- Account balances from bank sync are fixed. Bank history dated before an account's starting balance date was
  counted on top of a balance that already included it. That history is now kept as history and no longer changes
  the balance, so an account you made by hand and then linked shows the right balance after the update.
- The bank's balance now shows the date it was true, and the app warns when SimpleFIN's data for an account is
  more than a few days old.
- Delete account, on the account screen: removes the account and its transactions for good.
- Erase everything, in More: wipes all your data from the phone and starts over at the setup wizard. There's no
  backup yet, so it asks you to type ERASE first.
- Requires Android 13 or newer.
