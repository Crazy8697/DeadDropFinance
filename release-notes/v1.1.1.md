A budget start date, and a fix to the assigned total.

- Budget start date: bank history from before you started budgeting stays out of your envelopes, Ready to Assign and
  Safe to Spend, while still counting in Categories and in your account balances. It's set to the day you update
  (or the day setup finishes), and you can change it in More. Changing it never edits or deletes a transaction.
- The assigned total on Envelopes now counts income put straight into an envelope.
- Requires Android 13 or newer.
