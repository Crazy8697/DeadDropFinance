Tidier Home, and tax letters on receipts.

- Bonus income is in Home's top row, beside the sync status and Sync Now. Customize Home is in More, with the other settings, and Home no longer has a link for it.
- Receipts read the tax letter at the end of each item line as its own field, so it is never part of the item's name. The tax lines at the bottom are matched to the letters, and each item shows its price with its share of the tax. The items with tax add up to the receipt's total, and the form says so when they don't.
- When the letters can't be matched to the tax lines, the receipt form asks which items are taxed before it saves.
- Price history and a store's most bought items use the price with tax. Receipts saved before keep their shelf prices.
- Requires Android 13 or newer.
