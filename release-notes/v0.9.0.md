Receipts: scan them, match them to the charge, split by items, and return items.

- Scan a receipt from Activity, from a transaction (Add receipt), or by sharing a photo or PDF to the app. Take a
  photo or pick an image or PDF; long receipts can be several photos.
- The receipt is read on the phone, with nothing sent anywhere. You check the store, date, total, tax and items on
  a form under the photo; anything unclear is marked.
- A receipt is matched to its bank charge by total, date and store. If the charge hasn't come in yet, the receipt
  waits in More, Receipts and is matched when it does. Restaurant tips and fuel amounts are allowed for.
- Split by items: put each item in an envelope and the charge splits to match, tax included.
- Returns: when the purchase has a receipt, tick the items that came back and the money goes back to their
  envelopes.
- Receipt photos are stored encrypted in the app and deleted when you apply a split by items.
- Requires Android 13 or newer.
