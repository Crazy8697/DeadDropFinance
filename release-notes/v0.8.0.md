Sorting gets faster: suggestions, one-tap approve, spend alerts, returns and a balance check.

- Auto-categorize: new transactions come with a suggested envelope, from rules you make in More, Rules, or from
  where you put that payee before. Sort a payee by hand and the app offers to make a rule; three identical choices
  make one by themselves, which you can undo.
- Approve: in To sort, one tap approves a suggestion, picking an envelope changes it, and Approve all suggested
  takes the rest (it tells you how many first). Pending charges can be approved and stay approved when they post.
  Approving rule matches automatically is a setting, off by default.
- Spend alerts: after a background sync, a notification with the payee, amount, suggested envelope and what's
  left, plus warnings when an envelope runs low or overspent or Safe to Spend would go negative. The lock screen
  shows no amounts unless you turn that on. Alerts are only as live as bank sync.
- Returns: a refund from a store you bought from lately is suggested as a return and goes back to the purchase's
  envelopes. Any money in can be marked "This is a return".
- Balance check: each linked account's balance is checked against the bank's at the bank's date. A difference is
  shown plainly with an optional adjustment you confirm; a bank balance with the wrong sign is called out.
- Requires Android 13 or newer.
