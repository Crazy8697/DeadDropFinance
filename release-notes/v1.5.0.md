Pair by QR code, and take over editing.

- More > Server > Scan QR code: scan the code the desktop program shows under Settings > Server > Add a phone. The app checks the server's key against the code's fingerprint, then gets its own key with the one-time code (it works once, for 10 minutes). The QR code never holds a password or a key. The camera is asked for only when you tap Scan QR code; without it, type the address as before.
- Pairing works like a login: the server's budget replaces this phone's. If the phone has a budget of its own, an encrypted backup of it is saved to Downloads/DeadDropFinance first, with a password you type.
- Take over editing: while another device edits, the read-only banner has a Take over editing button. After a confirmation naming that device, the edit lock moves to this phone, and the other device turns read only with a banner saying who took over. Its changes that hadn't reached the server wait there and go once it can edit again (the later change wins, as always).
- The bank notification reader is gone, with its notification access, its settings, its screens and the spend alerts it posted. Transactions it already added stay as ordinary transactions. Its leftover settings are cleared when the app updates. CSV import, bill reminders and autopay warnings are unchanged.
- Requires Android 13 or newer.
