Payday and Fill envelopes on New transaction.

- Income going to Ready to assign can be ticked as Payday as you enter it: the new budget month starts on its date.
- Fill envelopes, under it, fills your envelopes from that payday in fill order. It can only be ticked once Payday is, and both are optional every time.
- Money in that is not a payday (a refund, say) stays plain money in.
- Mark as payday on a deposit's own screen works as before.
- Requires Android 13 or newer.
