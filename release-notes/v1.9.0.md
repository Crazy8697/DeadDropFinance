Reports, and an interest rate for Debt envelopes.

- Reports, in More: spending by envelope and by category, income vs expense, trends, money flow, a bills calendar, net worth, debt paydown, what the next payday covers, savings goals, merchant pages and price history. Each follows the budget month (Older and Newer), and tapping one opens it.
- A Debt envelope can have its interest rate (optional). With what's owed, Debt paydown shows the payoff date, the payments left and the interest on the way.
- Merchant pages and price history use your scanned receipts' items and the memos on purchases.
- Backup and restore no longer lists a cloud option that was never built.
- Settings switches keep a gap from their text.
- Updating keeps your budget. A phone on 1.8.2 or a program on 0.14.2 still syncs with this one, and leaves the interest rate as it is.
- Requires Android 13 or newer.
