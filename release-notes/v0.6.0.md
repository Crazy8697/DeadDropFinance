The first version you can budget with.

- Accounts: bank accounts, payment app balances and cash, each with a starting balance.
- Transactions: add spending, income and transfers by hand, split one across envelopes, mark one as pending, and
  move cash from an envelope when you take money out at an ATM.
- Envelopes: fill to a target, add every payday, save for a goal by a date, seasonal and debt envelopes, plus a
  Rollover envelope. Drag to set the order they fill in.
- Ready to Assign and the Assign screen: give every dollar a job, move money between envelopes.
- Payday: mark an income as payday. Leftover money in Fill to target envelopes goes to Rollover, then envelopes
  fill in order from what came in.
- CSV import: bring in a file exported from your bank or payment app. You see a preview with counts first, and
  rows already in the app are skipped.
- App lock: fingerprint or the phone's PIN when you open the app and after time away (5 minutes unless you change
  it). Hide screen keeps the app blank in recent apps, screenshots and screen mirroring.
- Your data is encrypted on the phone, with a key kept in the phone's secure hardware. Nothing about your budget
  is sent anywhere.
- There are no backups yet. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, your budget is gone. Backups come in a
  later version.
- Requires Android 13 or newer.
