Receipts read the way they do on Windows, and the receipt check is easier to follow.

- When the first reading of a receipt misses the balance due or the total tax, the picture is read a second time as scattered text and the amount that adds up is used. A small picture, such as a screenshot of a photo, is enlarged before it is read. Both come from the Windows program's reader, which the phone now shares.
- On the receipt check, Taxed and Not taxed are one switch: off is grey and says Not taxed, on is teal and says Taxed. Nothing beside it moves when you tap it. A switch the reader isn't sure of is outlined in yellow until you set it or mark the item Checked.
- A field the reader wasn't sure of has a yellow border and a yellow label. What to check is listed under the fields, one line each, starting with Check. That goes for Store and Date too. The yellow goes when you change the field or mark the item Checked.
- A price is pointed out as possibly misread only when the reader already had doubts about that line. An envelope chosen from the receipt's section is never shown in yellow.
- Changes to a saved receipt you are editing are kept until you save them or clear them. Clear on a saved receipt throws out only the unsaved changes.
- Every question the app asks before doing something (Clear this receipt, Delete, Log out and the rest) is one dialog: Cancel on the left, the action filled on the right, red when it throws something away. The wording is unchanged.
- A change of screen can no longer close the receipt check by mistake.
- Requires Android 13 or newer.
