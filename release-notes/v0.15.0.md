Backups, setup from a file, Purge data, the app's own icon, and a round of fixes.

- Backup and restore (More): one file with your budget, receipt photos and settings, locked with a password you
  pick, saved wherever you choose. Restore checks the whole file first and changes nothing if anything is wrong.
- Import setup (More): envelopes, bills, the next payday, rules, subscriptions and settings from a JSON file. It
  shows what will be added and updated first, never imports transactions and never deletes anything. The format is
  in docs/IMPORT.md.
- Purge data (More): clears all your data and settings but keeps your SimpleFIN connection, then starts at the setup
  wizard with bank sync connected. Erase everything is unchanged.
- Home lists debt envelopes with a payment due before payday, and warns when more is assigned than you have.
- The app has its icon, including a one-colour version for themed icons.
- About and Help screens, with credits, licenses, a privacy policy and an end user agreement.
- Fixes: a pending charge the bank drops no longer leaves its payday, bill payment or receipt behind; bank requests
  count every retry toward SimpleFIN's daily limit; a one-time SimpleFIN setup token is never sent twice; a photo
  or file picked with app lock set to Immediately isn't lost; rotating the phone keeps the screen; a second tap no
  longer saves twice; renaming the Cash envelope works; long PDFs are read a page at a time.
- Requires Android 13 or newer.
