Clearer Activity rows, and an overdrawn sign choice that sticks.

- Activity: each row shows the name, the date and status, and the category on three lines. Long names are cut with
  an ellipsis, and amounts line up in their own column.
- Bank sync: "It's actually negative (overdrawn)" now belongs to the bank account. It's offered wherever you link an
  account and on the account's screen, and it stays through Purge data, relinks and syncs.
- Requires Android 13 or newer.
