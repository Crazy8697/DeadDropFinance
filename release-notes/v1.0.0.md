Dead Drop Finance 1.0.0, the first full release.

What it does:

- Envelope budgeting: accounts, Ready to Assign, envelopes, paydays, bills and subscriptions, debt, cash and rollover.
- Bank transactions through SimpleFIN Bridge (your own account) or CSV import, or entered by hand; sorting with
  suggestions and rules, returns and refunds.
- Receipt scanning, split by items, and a merchant page.
- Safe to Spend, a balance forecast, parked buys, and alerts for new spending and bills.
- App lock and a hide-screen option. Your budget stays encrypted on your phone, with no account, ads or telemetry.
- Backup and restore in one encrypted file, a JSON setup importer, and updates that download and install from inside
  the app.
- Requires Android 13 or newer.
