Bills, paydays, subscriptions, a balance forecast, and spending from your bank app's notifications.

- Bills: a new Bills tab with what's coming up, overdue bills, and an autopay check. Bills can repeat monthly,
  every 3 or 6 months, or yearly, be paid in parts by different methods, and have their rest moved to a debt
  envelope. Bank sync and CSV import tick them paid. Yearly bills fill a little each payday.
- Payday: the next payday is predicted (and you can change it). When payday lands, a "Pay these now" card lists
  the bills due before the next one.
- Reminders, off by default and turned on per bill, plus autopay warnings, each with its own switch.
- Subscriptions: every repeating charge, monthly to yearly, with its latest price, price changes and a monthly
  total. Rename, merge, dismiss, or make one a bill.
- Balance forecast to payday on Home, with your plan and your usual pace, and the first day it would go below zero.
- Read bank notifications (off by default): spending shows up as soon as your bank app notifies you, and a payday
  deposit starts payday right away. Only the apps you pick are read, and only the amount and payee are kept.
- Receipt photos now stay after Split by items.
- Requires Android 13 or newer.
