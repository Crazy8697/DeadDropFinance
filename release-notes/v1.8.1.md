Edit a move, and parked buys removed.

- A move you made by hand (Move money, Assign, Cover) opens from an envelope's activity list to edit: its date, where the money came from and went, and the amount. Delete asks first, as a transaction's does. Edits and deletes sync. A payday's sweep and auto-fill and an ATM withdrawal's move stay as they are.
- Parked buys are gone: the screen, the Home card and the parking lot wait. A setup file can no longer set the wait.
- Backups made by the Windows program now carry receipt photos, and restore here with them.
- Requires Android 13 or newer.
