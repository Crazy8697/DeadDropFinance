Pick your currency, and restore without typing your key.

- More > Currency: one currency for the whole budget. The common ones come first (USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD, MXN, JPY, CHF, INR, NZD), then every currency, searchable by code or name. Amounts show and are typed the way that currency usually is, for example 1.234,56 € in euros. It also sets how CSV files are read when a file doesn't show it.
- It's display only: no exchange rates, nothing is fetched, and no amount changes. It syncs to your other devices through your server. A budget that never picked one stays in US dollars, and setup's first page offers the choice for a new one.
- Every currency shows two decimal places, since amounts are kept in hundredths.
- Restore now uses the recovery key saved on this phone when it opens the backup, so there's nothing to type. Only a backup made with a key this phone doesn't have asks for it.
- Requires Android 13 or newer.
