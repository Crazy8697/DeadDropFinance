Dead Drop Finance for Windows 0.15.8: a tidier item card on the receipt check screen.

- Each item is now two rows. The first has the item name and its envelope. The second has quantity, price, price with tax, a Taxed switch, and the Checked and Remove buttons. The quantity line ("2 @ $11.49 = $22.98") sits under them.
- Taxed is one switch, off grey and on teal, in place of the Taxed and Not taxed buttons. An item the program can't tell is taxed shows the switch off and highlighted; switch it on, or on and off again for not taxed.
- A field the reader wasn't sure of keeps a yellow border, and its label stays plain. What to check is listed in yellow on the right of the card, one line each ("Check price, may be misread"). In a narrower window the list goes under the fields.
- Opens budgets from 0.15.7 and keeps everything in them.
