Dead Drop Finance for Windows 0.15.11: tell the program when you're paid, and the next payday is right.

- Bills, Change payday now opens Payday schedule. Choose Monthly or Every two weeks.
- Monthly: the day of the month you're officially paid, how many days early your bank pays (0 to 5), and whether a payday on a weekend or holiday moves earlier or later. The popup shows your next three paydays as you set it, so you can check them.
- Every two weeks: your next payday, and the same weekend or holiday choice.
- Bank holidays are the Federal Reserve's, worked out for any year, with no internet needed.
- Once saved, the schedule sets the next payday for the forecast, Safe to spend, Pay these now, autopay checks and the late payday note. When you mark a deposit as payday, the next one is the schedule's next date. The program no longer guesses two weeks from a single payday.
- The schedule syncs to your other devices.
- Bills, Coming up shows this budget month: the bills due before your next payday, plus anything still unpaid from before, marked overdue. The card says how many are unpaid before payday.
- CSV import finds more of what's already in your budget: a transfer between your accounts, a split transaction, and a purchase you entered as one transaction per envelope. When it can't be sure, the row is unsure with the possible match beside it, never new.
- Venmo statements import: their own layout is recognized, the payee is who paid you or who you paid, Instant Transfers come in once with their fee, and a repayment that doesn't change your Venmo balance is listed under Not added. The same statement twice adds nothing.
- The import preview has a balance check: your bank's balance now (filled in from a Venmo statement, or typed), and what the account comes to after the import. If they're off, it says by how much and asks before importing.
- Opens budgets from 0.15.10 and keeps everything in them.
