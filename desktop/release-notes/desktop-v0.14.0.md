Dead Drop Finance for Windows 0.14.0: receipts read on this computer, one sidebar, and Edit a move.

- Receipts are read here, as on the phone: pick an image (JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF or BMP) or a PDF and the store, total and items fill in. The reading runs on this computer with Tesseract and the phone's English model, both inside the installer. Nothing is downloaded or sent. If it can't read one, type it in as before.
- Backups carry receipt photos, the same way the phone's do. A backup from either restores on the other with its photos; older backups without photos still restore.
- Hide screen, in Settings, Privacy (off at first, as on the phone): screenshots, screen recording and screen sharing show the window black.
- One sidebar: Budget, Activity, Accounts, Receipts, Bills, Categories, Reports, Sync and Settings. Home and More are gone.
- Budget now shows what Home did: the balance forecast, Pay these now, the over-assigned fix, and the Safe to Spend envelopes.
- Bills and Categories are laid out across the window, with the month in the top bar. Change payday is on Bills.
- Settings has what More had: Rules and auto-approve, Import CSV and Import setup, Alerts, Run setup again, Purge data, Erase everything and About.
- Edit a move: click a move you made by hand in Activity or an envelope's drawer to change its date, envelopes or amount, or delete it.
- Parked buys are gone.
- One sync status, in the header, with its Sync now.
- Updating from 0.13.0: the program offers this version in its update bar. Your budget and settings are kept.
