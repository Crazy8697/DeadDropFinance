Dead Drop Finance for Windows 0.15.2: tax letters on receipts.

- The receipt form reads the tax letter at the end of each item line as its own field, matches the tax lines at the bottom to the letters, and shows each item's price with its share of the tax. It says whether the items with tax add up to the receipt's total.
- When the letters can't be matched to the tax lines, the form asks which items are taxed before it saves.
- Price history and the Merchant pages use the price with tax. Receipts saved before keep their shelf prices.
- Opens budgets from 0.15.1 and keeps everything in them.
