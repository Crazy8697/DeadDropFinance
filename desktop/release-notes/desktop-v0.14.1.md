Dead Drop Finance for Windows 0.14.1: Receipts and the app's screens in the program's style.

- Receipts is laid out like the rest of the program: Waiting for a charge and Matched each in their own card across the window, and Scan is a button in the top bar.

- Activity's tabs on To sort spread across the window.
- Fixed: after Scan, a receipt that could match more than one charge now asks which one, instead of closing.
