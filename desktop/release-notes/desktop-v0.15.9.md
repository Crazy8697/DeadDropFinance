Dead Drop Finance for Windows 0.15.9: Store and Date match the item cards.

- On the receipt check screen, Store and Date are now compact boxes like the item boxes below them. Store lines up with the item names, Date is narrower with Today beside it. When the reader wasn't sure of one, its box is yellow and what to check is listed to the right, in the same column as the item cards' lists.
- In an item card the Envelope box now runs on to the right edge of the Remove button.
- The Taxed switch's label reads "Not taxed" when it is off and "Taxed" when it is on. Checked and Remove stay put when you click the switch.
- Opens budgets from 0.15.8 and keeps everything in them.
