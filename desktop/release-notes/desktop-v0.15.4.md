Dead Drop Finance for Windows 0.15.4: a clearer receipt check, and receipts read more reliably.

- Scanned receipts are read more reliably: section headers are never the store, quantity and weight lines keep their item's name, and the total and tax are found even when the photo blurs them.
- Each item on the receipt check is its own card: name, quantity, price, the price with tax beside it, Taxed or Not taxed, its envelope, and Checked above Remove.
- The totals show as the receipt does: untaxed items, taxed items, tax, and total paid. When they don't add up, the total says by how much, and any price that one misread digit would explain is pointed out.
- Anything the reader wasn't sure of has a yellow border and says why, until you change it or mark the item Checked.
- A receipt you are checking stays as you left it on another screen or after closing the program, until you save it or clear it.
- Split by items and item returns use each item's price with tax.
- Opens budgets from 0.15.3 and keeps everything in them.
