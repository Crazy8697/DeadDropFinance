Dead Drop Finance for Windows 0.15.6: confirms that look like the program, and scrollbars on every screen.

- Every question the program asks before doing something (Clear this receipt, Delete, Log out, Replace this budget and the rest) is now one dialog in the program's own style: a card like its others, over a dark layer that covers the whole window. Cancel is on the left; the action is the filled button on the right, red when it throws something away. Esc cancels, Enter goes ahead, and Cancel has the focus when it opens. The wording of each question is unchanged.
- Every screen that is taller than the window shows a thin scrollbar on the right, and only then. Content stays clear of it. A row that scrolls sideways gets one along its bottom.
- The second, scattered reading of a small receipt picture, which finds a faint total or total tax, is now part of the shared receipt reader. Receipts read the same as in 0.15.5.
- Opens budgets from 0.15.5 and keeps everything in them.
