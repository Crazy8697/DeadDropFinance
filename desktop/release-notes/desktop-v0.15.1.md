Dead Drop Finance for Windows 0.15.1: Reports at the program's text size, and fixes.

- The screens the program shares with the phone app (Reports, Rules, About and the others) now use the program's own text sizes, so their text matches the sidebar and the top bar.
- A report, an item, a store or any of those screens shows its name in the top bar after the screen's, with Back there, for example Reports, then Price history. No second title inside the page.
- Price history and a store's most bought items come only from scanned receipts. Transaction memos are no longer listed as items.
- The sidebar shows the Dead Drop Finance icon instead of a plain square.
- The File, View and Help menus highlight in the program's dark teal instead of blue.
- Updating from 0.15.0: the program offers this version in its update bar. Your budget and settings are kept.
