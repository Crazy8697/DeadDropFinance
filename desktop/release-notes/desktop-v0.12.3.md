Dead Drop Finance for Windows 0.12.3: edit and delete, and one copy at a time.

- Click a transaction in Activity, an account tab or an envelope's activity to edit it, with the same fields as the phone app. Delete asks first, as on the phone. Edits and deletes sync to your other devices.
- New: Transfer between your own accounts (an ATM withdrawal when the money goes into a cash account), Bonus income, Money in straight into an envelope, and Pending.
- New: Archive or Delete an account, Archive an envelope (with an Archived list to bring it back), Cover from Rollover, and changing the budget start date.
- After a CSV import, the program does what the phone does with the new transactions, such as matching bill payments.
- Only one copy of the program runs. Starting it again brings the open window to the front.
- Restore messages say what went wrong, without naming your file.
- Fixed: editing a debt envelope no longer clears its required payment and due date.
- Updating from 0.12.2: the program offers this version in its update bar. Your budget and settings are kept.
