Dead Drop Finance for Windows 0.7.0: the budget screens, and an encrypted budget.

- Budget: Ready to assign, Safe to spend, what was assigned and spent this budget month, and every envelope with its
  target, what's assigned, what's available and how full it is. Assign, Fill targets and New envelope.
- Click an envelope to see its activity on the right, with Add activity, Move money and Edit.
- Activity: All, plus a tab for each account. The All tab is view only; on an account's tab you can add a transaction
  or import a CSV export from your bank.
- Accounts: each account with its type, flags and balance, what's in the budget, what's tracked only, and the net.
- Add transaction and Move money from the top bar, and a budget month picker.
- Reports and Sync are placeholders for later versions. Sync says "Not connected": this computer's budget is its own
  until sync exists.
- Your budget on this computer is now encrypted in the same format as the phone's, with its key protected by Windows
  for your user account. The first time 0.7.0 starts, the unencrypted budget from 0.6.0 is copied into the encrypted
  one, checked, and only then removed. If that ever fails, the old file is kept and the program says so.
- Updating from 0.6.0: the program offers this version in its update bar. Your settings are kept.
