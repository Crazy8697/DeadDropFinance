Dead Drop Finance for Windows 0.15.7: every dialog in the program's style, and Enter presses the button you're on.

- In a question the program asks (Clear this receipt, Delete, Log out and the rest), Enter now presses the button that has the focus. The focus starts on Cancel, so Enter alone cancels; Tab moves to the other button. Esc still cancels.
- Every other dialog (Add transaction, Move money, New envelope, New account, Restore, Back up, Currency, Rename category and the rest) now looks like the questions do: a card like the program's others, over a dark layer that covers the whole window, Cancel on the left and the main button filled on the right. Esc closes them. Their fields and wording are unchanged, and Enter in a field does what it did before.
- Opens budgets from 0.15.6 and keeps everything in them.
