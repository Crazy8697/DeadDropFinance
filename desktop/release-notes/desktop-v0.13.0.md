Dead Drop Finance for Windows 0.13.0: everything the phone app does.

- New in the sidebar: Home, Bills, Categories and More. They are the phone app's own screens, with the same fields and wording.
- Home: Safe to Spend and choosing its envelopes, the over-assigned fix, Pay these now, the balance forecast and parked buys.
- Bills and subscriptions. Categories with Manage categories.
- More: receipts, CSV import with column mapping and a preview, Import setup, parked buys, rules and auto-approve, alerts, the setup wizard, Purge data, Erase everything and About.
- From the program's own screens: Fill order on Budget (drag with the mouse), To sort on Activity, and Details on a transaction or an envelope.
- Export CSV on Activity: what the tab shows for the budget month, saved where you pick.
- Bill reminders and autopay warnings show as Windows notifications.
- Receipts come from an image file (JPG or PNG): type in the store, the total and the items. The program has no camera and doesn't read the text.
- The update bar has Release notes and Skip this version.
- Updating from 0.12.3: the program offers this version in its update bar. Your budget and settings are kept.
