Dead Drop Finance for Windows 0.15.5: small receipt pictures read properly, and edits to saved receipts are kept.

- A small receipt picture, such as a screenshot of a photo, is enlarged before it is read, so every item, the tax and the total come through. When the total or the total tax is too faint in the first reading, the receipt is read again to find it.
- A price is pointed out as possibly misread only when the reader already had doubts about that line. Otherwise the total shows the gap and no item is marked.
- An envelope picked from the receipt's section is never shown in yellow.
- Changes to a saved receipt you are editing are kept through other screens and closing the program, until you save them or clear them. Clear on a saved receipt throws out only the unsaved changes.
- Opens budgets from 0.15.4 and keeps everything in them.
