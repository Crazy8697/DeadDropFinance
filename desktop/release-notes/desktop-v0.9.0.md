Dead Drop Finance for Windows 0.9.0: dark title bar, income splits, paydays.

- The window's title bar is dark, like the rest of the program (Windows' own title bar, in its dark mode).
- Money in can be split across envelopes, as on the phone: Split across envelopes, then each line goes to an envelope, Ready to assign or To sort.
- Payday without Fill envelopes now sweeps the month's leftovers into Rollover, as on the phone. With Fill envelopes it sweeps, then fills.
- The Sync screen says sync is coming: this program keeps its own budget on this computer for now.
- Updating from 0.8.0: the program offers this version in its update bar. Your budget and settings are kept.
