Dead Drop Finance for Windows 0.8.0: paydays, categories, splits, and backup.

- Add transaction has the phone's Payday and Fill envelopes boxes. Money in to Ready to assign can be ticked as Payday:
  the new budget month starts on its date. Fill envelopes, under it, fills your envelopes in fill order, and can only be
  ticked once Payday is. Both are optional every time. Money in that isn't a payday stays plain money in.
- A purchase needs a category, as on the phone. Picking one chooses the envelope that claims it (Unplanned when none
  does), and you can still change the envelope.
- Split across categories: one trip becomes lines, each with its own category, envelope and amount, and Left to split
  shows what's still to place.
- Not sorted yet (To sort) is now money in, left to sort later, as on the phone. In 0.7.0 it couldn't be saved.
- Backup and restore, in Settings and the File menu: one encrypted file, opened with a password you type twice. It's
  the same format as the phone's backups. A lost password means a lost backup: nothing keeps a copy of it. Restore asks
  for the file and its password and confirms before replacing your budget; a wrong password or a damaged file changes
  nothing.
- Updating from 0.7.0: the program offers this version in its update bar. Your budget and settings are kept.
