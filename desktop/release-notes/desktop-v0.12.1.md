Dead Drop Finance for Windows 0.12.1: currency list fix.

- The currency list now offers only currencies that use two decimal places, since amounts are kept in hundredths. Currencies without decimals (such as the Japanese yen) or with three (such as the Kuwaiti dinar) are no longer offered. A budget that already uses one keeps showing it and can change away from it.
- An envelope with no target now says "No target", as on the phone.
- Updating from 0.12.0: the program offers this version in its update bar. Your budget and settings are kept.
