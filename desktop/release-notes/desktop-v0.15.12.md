Dead Drop Finance for Windows 0.15.12: Bills one month at a time, a Payday button, and next month's budget before payday.

- Bills, Coming up shows one budget month at a time, payday to payday. Step to earlier or later months, or pick a custom range. The card's total and count follow what's shown.
- Each bill has a This month box: type what it really is this month (a higher electric bill, say). It counts for that month only, in the bill, the forecast, the bills calendar and the envelope's target for the month. Last checked shows when you last set it.
- Fixed: on a payday that wasn't marked yet, bills due after it could vanish from Coming up. They were never deleted; they show again.
- Payday button in the top bar: enter your bank balance now, after payday. If it's higher than the program shows, the difference is added as your payday deposit; if it's the same, your newest deposit is marked; if it's lower, it asks you to import or add the missing transactions first. Clean up (optional) zeroes every envelope through Unplanned first. One confirm shows everything before anything is saved.
- The Budget screen's month picker steps forward to next month's budget: assign money to it, move money and fill targets before payday. The same dollar can't be assigned to both months.
- Leftovers now carry into the new month, and overspending carries as negative. Nothing is swept into Rollover any more; Clean up zeroes envelopes when you want that.
- When a scheduled payday has come and isn't marked, Budget says "Payday was due Nov 27. Mark it with Payday." The month doesn't roll on its own.
- Opens budgets from 0.15.11 and keeps everything in them.
