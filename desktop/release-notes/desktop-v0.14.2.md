Dead Drop Finance for Windows 0.14.2: Settings laid out evenly, and a minimum window size.

- Settings shows its cards in three even rows of three, each row's cards the same width and height: General, Privacy, Backup and restore; Rules, Alerts, Import; Budget, Setup and data, About. Server stays across the window below them.
- The Budget card's start date and currency no longer sit against their Change buttons.
- The window can't be made smaller than 1100 x 700. It still opens at 1400 x 900 and can be made any size above that.
- At the smallest size, the envelope drawer's buttons wrap instead of squeezing, and dialogs fit the window, so Save and Cancel always show.
- Nothing in Settings changed except where it sits.
- Updating from 0.14.1: the program offers this version in its update bar. Your budget and settings are kept.
